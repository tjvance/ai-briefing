## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

**FCA: existing rules already apply to AI agents that handle money, and "tech provider" is no shield** [article] — https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/ai-agent-risks-face-action-fca/
On Nov 24 the UK FCA published an "Our approach to Agentic AI" page. It says current rules already cover agents that move money, senior managers are accountable for both built and bought AI, and tech firms whose agents handle payments could themselves be pulled into regulation. **Why it matters:** if you deploy an agent into a UK finance client, the accountable person is a named executive, so expect harder questions about audit trails and escalation. If you're the vendor, "we're just the tool" won't hold up.

**EU AI Act: Annex III high-risk deadline is now Dec 2, 2027 (correction to the July brief)** [article] — https://obligara.com/news/2026-11-13-eu-ai-act-digital-omnibus-delay
The Digital Omnibus (Reg. 2026/1744, in force since Jul 27) moved stand-alone high-risk obligations, which cover credit scoring and employment, from Aug 2, 2026 to Dec 2, 2027. Embedded systems moved to Aug 2028. Article 5 bans and Article 50 transparency duties did not move. **Why it matters:** the July brief's "Aug 2 hard deadline" for EU finance is out of date. Clients have more runway on high-risk systems, but transparency duties for chatbots and generated content already apply.
