## 1. Frontier Labs

- [Replit switches its default Agent provider from Anthropic to OpenAI](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/17/replit-is-no-longer-working-with-anthropic-ceo-says/) [article] — Cites cost, performance, and partnership; users can still pick Claude. For SaidnDone and client work: a large channel customer can flip default providers overnight, so keep model-agnostic abstractions.
- [How AI is reshaping Anthropic's workforce](https://www.anthropic.com/research/employment-at-anthropic) [article] — Junior technical hires fell from 27% to 9% of technical hires since early 2025; work shifts to supervising agents. Useful framing for FDE/PM interviews on where entry-level and mid-level roles go.
- [How I AI: Meta's Muse Wonder is good for agents, bad for design](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-i-ai-meta-muse-wonder-how-brooke) [podcast] — Hands-on test across ~20 tasks; strongest at billing-ticket resolution, inbox triage, computer use, and resisted a prompt-injection plant. A concrete agent eval pattern you can reuse with clients.

## 2. Frontier Research

- [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017) [article] — Open weights; cross-layer KV reuse plus FP4 KV caching cuts the persistent KV footprint to roughly 1/8 of V4-Flash. Self-hosting economics for clients who can't use US APIs.
- [Mercury 3 (Inception Labs)](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-3) [article] — Diffusion language model reportedly beating GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 on several benchmarks at 1,000+ tokens/s (vendor claim). Worth watching for latency-bound agent steps.
- [AI Benchmarks: Climbing the Wrong Hills](https://research.contrary.com/report/ai-benchmarks-climbing-the-wrong-hills) [article] — Argues benchmaxxing distorts what models are built to do. Supports the FDE line that client-specific evals beat leaderboards.

## 3. Applied AI, Engineering & Customer-Facing Deployment

- [Building Laminar: Ramp's managed agents platform](https://builders.ramp.com/post/building-laminar) [article] — Model-agnostic harness on Postgres, Temporal, and S3; session state lives apart from disposable sandboxes; v1 built in six weeks. A reference architecture for durable agents in a client environment (and it maps well to D365 batch/workflow thinking).
- [How the Claude Code team does code review](https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/) [article] — Continuous review, automated verification, tiered risk-based approvals. Borrow the tiering for agent-change governance in regulated clients.
- [Nate's Newsletter: Your agents need their own computer](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/computer-for-ai-agents) [video] — 27-minute Substack video (paywalled in part) on auditing what agents leave running and sizing hardware. Lower priority; useful for on-prem/edge deployment conversations.
