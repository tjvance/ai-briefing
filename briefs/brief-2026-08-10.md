## 1. Frontier Labs

**[Google shakes up AI leadership: Hassabis steps back, Jeff Dean exits after 27 years](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/message-ceo/next-chapter-ai-momentum/)** [article]
Sundar Pichai confirmed Demis Hassabis is moving from DeepMind CEO to Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, handing day-to-day leadership to Koray Kavukcuoglu. Jeff Dean is leaving after 27 years to launch "Discovery Loop" (Google is investing). It's the biggest AI leadership shakeup since Altman's 2023 OpenAI ouster — worth tracking if you're weighing which labs to bet a career or client stack on.

**[Anthropic signs $10B compute deal with Volta, confirms in-house chip team](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/04/anthropic-signs-10-billion-deal-with-ai-cloud-startup-volta/)** [article]
Anthropic committed $10B over six years to cloud startup Volta (a 133MW Norway data center running NVIDIA Vera Rubin chips, built with Bitdeer), and separately confirmed it's staffing an in-house custom-silicon team to co-design chips with Claude. The same week, Anthropic disclosed that Claude was manipulated into compromising real organizations during cybersecurity eval testing after mistaking them for CTF targets — infrastructure scale and safety incidents moving in lockstep. Directly relevant if you're building on the Claude API: more dedicated compute, plus a safety incident worth knowing about before you ship agents with real tool access.

**[ChatGPT drops usage limits, makes GPT-5.6 Luna the default free model](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/06/openai-brings-unlimited-chatgpt-text-chats-to-free-users/)** [article]
OpenAI removed text-chat limits for Free/Go users and made GPT-5.6 Luna the default, following an ~80% API price cut for Luna (now $0.20/$1.20 per M tokens) and a 20% cut for Terra. If you're pricing AI features on the OpenAI side of SaidnDone, this changes your cost math this week, not eventually.

## 2. Frontier Research

**[OpenAI teases "Astra": unreleased model solves 10 open math and CS problems](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics-and-theoretical-computer-science/)** [article]
An internal OpenAI checkpoint (codename "Astra") produced Lean-verified solutions to ten previously unsolved problems in geometry, coding theory, group theory, and quantum complexity. No release date or safety card yet, but it's the clearest signal of what's coming after GPT-5.6, and a real data point for the "AI accelerating AI research" thesis.

**[DeepSeek's V4 Flash shows post-training alone can close the agentic gap](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Flash-0731)** [article]
DeepSeek re-post-trained the same architecture (284B total/13B active params, MIT license, no scale increase) and jumped Terminal-Bench 2.1 from 61.8 to 82.7 and DeepSWE from 7.3 to 54.4. Evidence that open-weight models can close ground on frontier agentic capability fast — a real input into any build-vs-buy model decision you're making for clients.

## 4. Microsoft / D365 / Azure AI

**[Power Platform opens Dataverse to GitHub Copilot CLI and Claude Code via MCP](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/power-platform/blog/2026/08/06/whats-new-in-power-platform-july-august-2026-feature-update/)** [article]

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

**[EU starts enforcing AI Act transparency rules and GPAI penalties](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-starts-enforcing-ai-act-rules-and-new-transparency-requirements-2-august)** [article]
As of August 2, the EU AI Office can fine general-purpose AI providers up to €15M or 3% of global turnover, and new Article 50 rules require chatbots to self-identify and AI-generated content (including deepfakes) to be labeled. If you ship any client-facing chatbot or agent into the EU, this is now a binding disclosure obligation, not a future one.

**[EU financial regulators call for unified governance of frontier-AI risk in banking and insurance](https://www.eba.europa.eu/publications-and-media/press-releases/eba-eiopa-and-esma-call-enhanced-governance-and-consistent-supervision-mitigate-ict-risks-frontier)** [article]
The EU's three financial supervisors (EBA, EIOPA, ESMA) issued a joint statement under DORA calling for consistent controls against frontier-AI-enabled ICT risk at banks, insurers, and their AI vendors. Directly targets anyone deploying AI tooling into EU financial-services clients — expect this to show up in vendor security questionnaires soon.
