## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

**Anthropic details Claude's new text watermarking** [article] — https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-text-watermark
Claude now embeds an imperceptible, persistent watermark in generated text (SynthID-Text-style) across the API, Claude Code, and Cowork, plus a forthcoming detection API — direct compliance with the EU AI Act's Article 50 transparency mandate. Caveat worth knowing: the watermark proves text was *processed* by Claude, not that a human didn't write it — proofreading and translation get watermarked too. **Why it matters:** don't rely on this alone as an AI-disclosure control in client deliverables without understanding what it actually proves.

**EU AI Act enforcement powers go live; OpenAI, Anthropic, Google now directly in scope** [article] — https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/03/eu-ai-act-enforcement-powers.html
The EU AI Office can now demand technical documentation, evaluate models pre-release, restrict EU market access, and fine up to €15M or 3% of global turnover for any general-purpose-AI provider serving the EU — regardless of where the provider is headquartered. **Why it matters:** documentation and disclosure gaps in a client's AI deployment are now fine-able, not theoretical, if there's any EU exposure. No new finance-sector-specific US rule (SEC/OCC/Fed) surfaced this week — existing April 2026 guidance remains the operative baseline.
