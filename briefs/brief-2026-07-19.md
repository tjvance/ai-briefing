## 2. Frontier Research

**SEED: Self-Evolving On-Policy Distillation for Agentic Reinforcement Learning** [article] — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.14777
A policy model mines its own completed agent trajectories for reusable "hindsight skills" in natural language, then distills them back into itself during RL — targeting the sparse-reward problem in long-horizon, tool-using agent training. **Why it matters:** this is the technical machinery behind "agents that get better at your specific workflow over time," the same theme last week's Autoresearch item pointed at — good vocabulary for describing agent-reliability roadmaps to clients.

**SearchOS-V1: Robust Open-Domain Information-Seeking Agent Collaboration** [article] — https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.15257
Treats open-domain information-seeking as a scheduling problem: sub-agents share a "Coverage Map," "Evidence Graph," and "Failure Memory" so they stop repeating each other's search loops, with pipeline-parallel scheduling for throughput. **Why it matters:** a concrete architecture pattern for multi-agent research/RAG systems — directly applicable if you're designing agent-to-agent collaboration for a client rather than one mega-agent.

## 3. Applied AI, Engineering & Customer-Facing Deployment

**5 Trends That Defined AI Engineering at World's Fair 2026** [article] — https://www.latent.space/p/aiewf26trends
Latent Space's wrap-up argues the center of gravity has shifted from "building with agents" to "building systems around agents" — harnesses, context, permissions, and eval infrastructure now matter as much as model choice. Full agent autonomy was treated as undesirable at scale, with most client-specific work happening at the orchestration layer, and MCP is now assumed infrastructure rather than a headline feature. **Why it matters:** this is a direct description of the FDE job — the orchestration/harness layer is exactly where you'd sit in a client engagement.

**How Anthropic runs large-scale code migrations with Claude Code** [article] — https://claude.com/blog/ai-code-migration
Anthropic's internal migration playbook, including a case where Claude Code ported ~1M lines of Bun from Zig to Rust in under two weeks with 100% of existing tests passing pre-merge, and a 165K-line Python-to-TypeScript migration using phase gates and adversarial review rounds. Core insight: fix the process/loop that produced the code, not the code itself. **Why it matters:** a ready-made case study for FDE/consultant conversations about legacy migrations — directly relevant if a D365 F&O client ever asks "could AI help with our X++ modernization."

**Zero risk isn't the job: a CISO's guide to agentic AI** [article] — https://claude.com/blog/ciso-guide-to-agentic-ai
Anthropic's Deputy CISO lays out a four-question framework for gating agent deployments: what content does it ingest, what actions can it take, what's the blast radius if it's wrong, and can you observe what it did — arguing the goal is "legible and bounded" risk, not zero risk. **Why it matters:** close to a ready-made answer for "how do you think about AI risk with enterprise clients" in an interview, and a framework you can bring straight into an FDE/consultant engagement.

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

**EU AI Act: what actually applies on August 2, 2026** [article] — https://www.technology.org/2026/07/17/eu-ai-act-what-actually-applies-on-2-august-2026/
Clarifies what actually goes live on the Aug 2 milestone: Article 50 transparency obligations become enforceable (chatbots must disclose they're AI in the first exchange, AI-generated/deepfake content must be labeled), and the EU AI Office gains full penalty powers over general-purpose-AI model providers. The Digital Omnibus deal pushed the harder stuff — high-risk Annex III obligations — out to December 2027/August 2028. **Why it matters:** two weeks out, this is the concrete "what changes on Aug 2" list to bring to any client with EU exposure — chatbot disclosure and deepfake labeling are live now; the high-risk-system rules you were tracking got more runway.

**AI Governance Weekly: dual compliance deadlines + DHS-CISA agentic AI guidance** [article] — https://aigovernance.com/news/ai-governance-weekly-july-16-2026
Flags China's Implementation Opinions on intelligent agents (effective July 15) landing alongside Illinois' third-party frontier-model audit mandate as near-simultaneous compliance deadlines, plus a DHS-CISA analysis pushing for mandatory prompt-injection protections and human-override documentation for agentic AI in critical infrastructure. **Why it matters:** the DHS-CISA prompt-injection/human-override language previews what "secure agentic deployment" documentation will be expected to look like in regulated US sectors — worth having an answer ready if a client asks.
