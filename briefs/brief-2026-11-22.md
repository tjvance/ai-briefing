## 1. Frontier Labs

- **[Introducing GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-2-astra-sol/)** [article]
  Released Nov 17 in the API as `gpt-6.2-astra` ($8/$40 per M tokens, about 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra) and `gpt-6.2-sol` ($2/$10). Benchmarks look strong, but early testers report loops and tasks declared done too early, and the new cyber safeguards can block legitimate security work. *For SaidnDone:* run your own evals before switching defaults and don't trust the launch numbers. *(Surfaced by 6+ outlets.)*
- **[Introducing Gemini 4 Neon](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-neon/)** [article]
  Google's mid-tier model shipped Nov 18. It costs $3/$15 per M tokens, cached input is 95% cheaper, and the context window is 1M tokens. Google reports it topping Zapier's AutomationBench and Vending-Bench 2. *Why it matters:* it's priced well for agent workloads with long, repeated context (for example, meeting transcripts plus a system prompt). Add it to your multi-provider routing tests next to Claude and GPT-6.2 Sol. *(Surfaced by 5+ outlets.)*
- **[Anthropic closes its IPO](https://www.anthropic.com/news/ipo-close)** [article]
  The IPO priced at $83 on Nov 18, above the $70–78 range. ANT started trading on Nasdaq Nov 19, and the deal closed Nov 20 with the underwriters' option fully exercised. Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 (Nov 9) remain Anthropic's top models. *Why it matters:* the vendor you build on now reports quarterly, so expect more discipline on pricing and deprecations. "Anthropic as a public company" is also a ready talking point for consulting and PM interviews. *(Surfaced by 8+ outlets.)*

## 3. Applied AI, Engineering & Customer-Facing Deployment

- **[Building Legal Agents When You Can't See the Data — Jacob Lauritzen, Legora (Latent Space)](https://latent.space/p/building_legal_agents_when_you_can_t_see_the_data_-_jacob_lauritzen_cto_of_legora/)** [podcast]
  Legora can't read customer agent traces because the data is confidential, so it rebuilds client work as RL environments (for example, a 10,000-file due-diligence data room). It covers OpenAI's deployment company and Tomoro acquisition, and Anthropic's financial-crimes agent with FIS. *Why it matters:* this is the market behind your FDE interview. Use it for "why FDE, why now" answers. *(Not new this week; included as interview prep.)*

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

- **[EU AI Act watermarking grace period ends December 2, 2026 (CSA research note)](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-article50-watermarking-deadline/)** [article]
  Generative systems already on the market before Aug 2, 2026 have **10 days left** to meet the Art. 50(2) marking and detection duty. Chatbot disclosure and the other Art. 50 duties got no extension. *Why it matters:* any client deployment that outputs generated content to EU users needs a marking plan now. That includes Copilot-drafted documents and SaidnDone summaries.
- **[The AI Act delay: what the Digital Omnibus actually changes for banks](https://www.regulation-dora.eu/blog/ai-act-omnibus-delay-what-it-changes-for-banks-2026)** [article]
  High-risk obligations for credit scoring and insurance pricing moved from Aug 2026 to **Dec 2, 2027**, and transparency duties still apply. *Why it matters:* finance clients get breathing room on high-risk compliance but not on disclosure. Expect "we can pilot now, but design for 2027" conversations.
