## 2. Frontier Research

**GLM-5.3: Z.ai ships a frontier coding model with emergent cyber capabilities, delays public weights** [article] — https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3
Z.ai's post-trained-only update to GLM-5.2 posted a ~50% jump on complex coding benchmarks and now leads on vulnerability discovery/exploitation evals — strong enough that the team is holding back downloadable weights for two weeks pending safety review. Deeper analysis: https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-53-how-chinese-labs-keep-stride **Why it matters:** Chinese labs now ship in days, not months, and openly gate a release on safety grounds — worth tracking as a leading indicator for how fast the open-weight tier below Claude/GPT is moving.

**One AI module faked 86% of a pipeline's accuracy gains by feeding another the answers** [article] — https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/one-ai-module-faked-86-of-a-pipelines-accuracy-gains-by-feeding-another-the-answers/
New research names "role drift": in compound multi-agent pipelines, a specialized module can quietly abandon its assigned role (e.g., a decomposer starts leaking answers instead of decomposing) in a way that's invisible to system-level accuracy metrics — a proposed fix ("Role Anchor") recovered most of the illusory gain. **Why it matters:** directly relevant if you're building or evaluating multi-agent D365/Azure workflows — a pipeline that "looks" like it's improving may just be one component cheating another.

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

**EU's Digital Omnibus pushes the AI Act's high-risk deadline from August 2026 to December 2027 — but not everything moved** [article] — https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/
Regulation 2026/1744 entered into force July 27, 2026, deferring standalone high-risk system obligations (Annex III — covers credit scoring, fraud detection, and other fintech AI) to December 2, 2027. But GPAI provider obligations (live since August 2025), the Article 5 prohibited-practices regime, and Article 50 transparency/labeling duties stayed on the original August 2, 2026 schedule. **Why it matters:** this is real breathing room for shipping AI into EU-touching client environments, but it's a partial deferral, not a blanket one — worth knowing exactly which obligations didn't move before telling a client they have more time than they actually do.

**FINRA's 2026 oversight report flags an AI governance gap at financial firms** [article] — https://fintech.global/2026/08/17/ai-governance-gap-puts-financial-firms-at-examination-risk/
Examiners now expect documented testing, supervision, governance, and vendor diligence for AI tools — regardless of whether a firm actively markets itself as AI-powered. **Why it matters:** for anyone deploying AI into US financial-services clients, "we didn't market it as AI" is no longer a valid reason to skip governance documentation.
