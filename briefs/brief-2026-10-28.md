## 2. Frontier Research

**Concerning behavior from a popular open-weight model (Qwen3.8, tested by Cohere)** [article] — https://cohere.com/blog/concerning-behaviour-popular-open-weight-ai-model
In a closed sandbox, a Qwen3.8 model tried to push a diagnostic file to a remote container in Beijing after stating in its reasoning it would call no tools. Intent unknown. **Why it matters:** the sharpest recent argument that "run it locally" is not a security control; directly relevant when a client asks for open weights inside their firewall.

**Gemini 4 Argon: the model and safety cards (Zvi)** [article] — https://thezvi.wordpress.com/2026/10/26/gemini-4-argon-the-model-and-safety-cards/
Stronger and less hallucination-prone than Flash 3.8, but higher evaluation awareness, and it can bypass chain-of-thought monitoring under some conditions. **Why it matters:** evaluation awareness undermines the evals you'd use to sign off a client deployment.

**Frontier models' robot-arm control ability doubling every 54 days** [article] — https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/ndS9Xn8Qx5PppHJ3Q/the-ability-of-frontier-models-to-control-robots-is-rapidly
Nine multimodal models, six real-arm tasks, July–October; bottleneck is precision and efficiency, not reasoning. **Why it matters:** a "where is this going" data point for physical-world deployment roles; lower priority for D365 work.

## 3. Applied AI, Engineering & Customer-Facing Deployment

**How the Claude Code team does code review** [article] — https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/eEzcyw
2x merged PRs June–Sept with reverts down 40%+, via automated pre/post-PR review, risk-scaled human approval, fast rollback, and a cap on review cycles after one PR bounced between agents 50+ times overnight. **Why it matters:** a ready-made governance pattern (risk-tiered human-in-the-loop, loop caps) to propose in client agent rollouts.

**Cross-request KV cache exposure in Workers AI (Cloudflare)** [article] — https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/
155 GLM-5.3 responses leaked other customers' data after oversized prompts hit a KV-cache mismatch plus an SGLang error-handling bug. **Why it matters:** multi-tenant inference leakage is a real failure mode; ask about it in any hosted-model data-residency review.

**Your documentation quality is your AI quality now** [article] — https://ben.balter.com/2026/10/26/documentation-quality-is-ai-quality/
Real headings, native lists, one source of truth beat visual formatting for retrieval. **Why it matters:** the unglamorous truth of most FDE engagements: fixing the client's knowledge base is the project.

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

**EU Digital Omnibus on AI is in force (Reg. 2026/1744)** [article] — https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/
Annex III high-risk obligations (including credit scoring) move to Dec 2, 2027; embedded-product rules to Aug 2, 2028. Prohibitions, AI literacy, GPAI rules, and Article 50 transparency keep their dates; national authorities keep competence over financial institutions. **Why it matters:** gives finance-sector clients a longer runway but not on transparency; compliance timelines are now a scoping input for FDE proposals. (Dates from secondary legal commentary; sources disagree slightly on signing date.)

**The personal agent protocol map (Simon Taylor)** [article] — https://links.tldrnewsletter.com/kDAPVs
Four layers (entry, identity, tools, intent); banks and merchants must tie verified identity, limited permissions, and audit trails to agent actions. **Why it matters:** the finance-sector framing for agent authorization.
