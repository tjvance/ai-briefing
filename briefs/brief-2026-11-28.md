## 1. Frontier Labs

- **[Nov 30 API deadline wave: OpenAI Agent Builder, v1/prompts, and hosted Evals shut down](https://platform.openai.com/docs/deprecations)** [article]
  Agent Builder, reusable prompt objects (`v1/prompts`), and the hosted Evals platform all go dark **Monday, Nov 30**. gpt-5/o3 snapshots follow Dec 11, and gpt-image-1-mini/1.5 on Dec 1 ([tracker summary](https://brco.dev/insights/updates/openai-api-deprecations-2026/)). On the same day, **Claude Sonnet 4.5** (`claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929`) retires from the Claude API ([Anthropic deprecations](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/resources/model-deprecations)). **For SaidnDone:** move any server-side prompts into code and grep for pinned IDs before Monday. It's also a good FDE story: vendor-hosted orchestration is a lock-in risk you should design around for clients.
- **[Gemini app splits chat history from AI training](https://androidcentral.com/apps-software/ai/you-can-now-keep-your-gemini-history-without-feeding-googles-training-pipeline)** [article]
  The Gemini app now has separate controls for keeping history and for allowing training, rolling out over the coming weeks. Work and school accounts can't change these settings. This is a consumer feature, but client data-governance teams ask exactly this question ("is our data training the model?"), and the answer differs between consumer and enterprise tiers across all three labs.

*Also noted (unverified):* Claude Duet's US waitlist has reportedly cleared for all Pro/Max/Ultra subscribers, but it's still a US-only research preview ([rollout status](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/claude-duet-release-date-and-rollout-status)). It's a proactive-agent product next to SaidnDone.

## 2. Frontier Research

- **[NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Parse](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-parse/modelcard)** [article]
  An open document parser under 1B parameters that outputs text, semantic layout classes, bounding boxes, and reading order. NVIDIA says it's #1 on ParseBench in its size class with 4.6× PaddleOCR-VL's throughput. For D365 work, this is a cheap, self-hostable front end for invoice, PO, and vendor-document ingestion (the core of most AP-automation pilots), and it can run on-tenant via Foundry on AKS.
- **[Interconnects — "Open-source AI without Jensen"](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/interconnects/id1719552353)** [podcast]
  Nathan Lambert argues that Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia removes the main US backer of open-weight models, and that the open ecosystem's best lane is enterprise post-training and "ownership of intelligence." That's directly relevant when a client asks whether to bet on an open model inside their own tenant.

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

- **[Banks are shutting out outside AI agents while the open-banking rule stalls](https://captaincompliance.com/education/banks-restrict-ai-agent-account-access/)** [article]
  Wells Fargo's updated access agreement took effect Nov 24. It treats AI-agent actions as customer-authorized, "at your own risk," and Citi, Chase, BofA, PNC, and Citizens have restricted or warned about third-party agents. CFPB 1033 is still stalled. Any agent that touches bank or payment flows (AP automation from D365 included) now needs explicit authorization and liability design (unverified; check the bank agreements).
- **[Bloomberg November 2026 Global Regulatory Brief: agentic AI](https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/insights/regulation/november-2026-global-regulatory-brief-agentic-ai-stablecoins-and-digital-money/)** [article]
  Notable: the HKMA's new "ART" (Accountability, Resilience, Trust) agentic-AI governance framework for banks replaces its 2019 principles. Together with the FCA (Nov 25 brief) and the OCC, finance regulators are converging on agent identity, accountability, and resilience. Build that checklist into every regulated deployment.

*Reminder:* the EU AI Act's new prohibitions and the Art. 50(2) watermarking grace period end **Dec 2**, four days from now.
