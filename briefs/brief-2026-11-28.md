## 1. Frontier Labs

- **[Claude Sonnet 4.5 retires from the Claude API on Nov 30, 2026](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/resources/model-deprecations)** [article]
  `claude-sonnet-4-5-20250929` stops working **in 2 days**. The replacement is `claude-sonnet-5-5`, and Opus 4.5 follows on Jan 22, 2027. **Action for SaidnDone:** grep for pinned 4.5 IDs today. Bedrock, Vertex, and Foundry run their own retirement schedules.
- **[OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 Astra & Sol; Nov 30 shutdown wave](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/11/17/openai-gpt-6-2-astra-sol-release/)** [article]
  GPT-6.2 launched Nov 17. Astra is $8/$40 per 1M tokens, 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra. Sol is $2/$10. Separately, Agent Builder, the `v1/prompts` reusable prompts, and the hosted Evals platform all shut down **Nov 30**, and gpt-5/o3 snapshots follow Dec 11 ([deprecations page](https://platform.openai.com/docs/deprecations)). If SaidnDone stores prompts server-side at OpenAI, move them into code now.
- **[Anthropic launches Claude Meetings](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/)** [article]
  Claude now joins video calls as a visible participant and turns the discussion into plans, whiteboards, and tasks. It's in beta for Team and Enterprise. This is a frontier lab moving directly into SaidnDone's category, so your differentiation story needs to be sharp: vertical depth, ERP and action integration, and data residency.

*Also noted:* Google released Gemini 4 Neon on Nov 18, pitched as Carbon-class quality at Flash speed ([9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/11/18/gemini-4-neon-announcement/)). Claude Fable 5.2 shipped Nov 9 for long-running agentic work.

## 2. Frontier Research

- **[NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Parse](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/nemotron-3.5-parse/modelcard)** [article]
  An open document parser under 1B parameters that outputs text, layout classes, bounding boxes, and reading order. NVIDIA says it ranks #1 on ParseBench in its size class. For D365 work, this is a cheap self-hostable front end for invoice, PO, and vendor-document ingestion, which is the unglamorous core of most AP-automation pilots.
- **[Interconnects — "Open-source AI without Jensen"](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/interconnects/id1719552353)** [podcast]
  Nathan Lambert argues that Jensen Huang's exit from Nvidia removes the main US sponsor of open-weight models, and that the open ecosystem's best lane is enterprise post-training and "ownership of intelligence." That matters when a client asks whether they can run an open model inside their own tenant.

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

- **[EU AI Act Omnibus: high-risk deadlines postponed](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)** [article]
  Annex III high-risk obligations move to Dec 2, 2027, and Annex I embedded products to Aug 2, 2028. What doesn't move: prohibitions (two new ones apply **Dec 2, 2026**), AI-literacy duties, GPAI obligations, and Article 50 transparency rules. Article 50 matters for meeting bots: a bot that joins EU calls needs disclosure now.
