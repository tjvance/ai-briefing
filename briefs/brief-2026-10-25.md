## 2. Frontier Research

- **[LLMs are making formal verification feasible at scale](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formal-verification)** [article] — Claude produced machine-checked proofs for 239 fuzz-test entry points across 123 open-source libraries. The guarantees are scoped: inputs under 64 MB, single-threaded model. The takeaway for you is that tests-as-specs plus model-generated proofs is where assurance for agent-written code is heading. That's a credible answer to "how do you trust what the agent shipped" in an interview.

## 3. Applied AI, Engineering & Customer-Facing Deployment

- **[Building agents: when to fold subagents into your main agent loop](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-agents-when-to-fold-subagents-into-your-main-agent-loop)** [article] — Anthropic's guidance: collapsing routers, planners and specialists into one loop cuts lossy handoffs, cost and latency when skills and tool search load context on demand. Keep separate agents for parallel work, verification, permission boundaries and real context overload. Use it as an architecture checklist for client agent designs.
- **[Forward Deployed Engineering 101 — Kevin Bai](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KwhgfwOSToQ)** [video] — Talk by an Anthropic engineer who was a founding FDE at Rippling and worked at Palantir. It argues FDEs are what unlock large contract values. Watch before the FDE interview. *(Description only; not watched.)*

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

- **[FSB urges more human oversight of agentic AI](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/10/16/business/foreign-business/fsb-urges-more-oversight-of-agentic-ai/2446909)** [article] — Final FSB guidance (Oct 15) calls for risk-based triggers for when an agent must seek human approval in higher-risk use. US state regulators' CSBS framework (Sep 16) similarly asks examiners to look at permission boundaries, logging, reversibility and kill-switches. In finance engagements, design approval gates and audit logs in from the start.
- **[EU AI Act Omnibus: postponed high-risk deadlines](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)** [article] — High-risk (Annex III) obligations moved to Dec 2, 2027, but Article 50 transparency duties still apply. That includes text watermarking, which Anthropic's latest Claude models now embed to comply (per [O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/radar/the-latest-anthropic-claude-models-contain-ai-text-watermarks-what-does-this-mean-for-you)). Sources I found disagreed on timing, so confirm against the Official Journal before advising a client.
- **[A LiteLLM release carried credential-stealing malware — Nate's Newsletter](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/ai-agents-cybersecurity)** [video] — 26-minute walkthrough of the Oct 21 LiteLLM v1.107.0 PyPI compromise (live about 2 hours) and why stolen AI provider keys are valuable. If SaidnDone or any client gateway uses LiteLLM, rotate keys and check the build logs. Most of the post is paywalled.
