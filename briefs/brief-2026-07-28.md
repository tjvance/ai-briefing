# AI Intelligence Briefing — July 28, 2026

*Covering July 25–28, 2026. Curated for an ERP/D365 tech lead moving into applied-AI, FDE, and AI product roles.*

## 1. Frontier Labs

- **[Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5)** [article] — Opus 5 approaches Fable 5's capability at roughly half the price and became the default model for Claude Max, leading several coding and knowledge-work benchmarks. Directly relevant to SaidnDone's cost/quality tradeoff: this is the model tier to re-benchmark your Anthropic API calls against before your next interview.
- **[Nvidia in talks for up to $250B backstop to fund OpenAI's Ohio data center](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/nvidia-and-openai-in-talks-for-up-to-250-billion-dollar-ai-backstop.html)** [article] — Nvidia is discussing guaranteeing debt financing for a 10GW OpenAI campus, on top of a separate $350B chip-purchase deal already under negotiation. Signals just how capital-intensive frontier compute has become — a data point for any "where is this going" conversation in an FDE or AI PM interview.
- **[OpenAI launches Health in ChatGPT for US adults](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-health/)** [article] — Connects Apple Health and records from Epic, Oracle Health, One Medical, and others; OpenAI says health data won't train models or target ads. Useful as a template for how a frontier lab handles a regulated-data vertical — relevant to any client conversation about connecting Claude/GPT to sensitive enterprise data (D365, HR, finance).

## 2. Frontier Research

- **[Kimi K3 Redraws the Open Frontier](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/issue-363)** [article] — Moonshot's 2.8T-parameter Kimi K3 lands just behind GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Fable 5 on major benchmarks and will ship as the largest open-weights model to date. The Batch's Andrew Ng also covers the OpenAI-agent-hacks-Hugging-Face incident in the same issue — worth reading for the "open models helped defend, not just attack" argument that's reshaping the open-weight policy debate below.
- **[The Open-Weight Unbundling](https://insights.euclid.vc/p/the-open-weight-unbundling-jake-saper-emergence-capital)** [article] — VC analysis arguing open-weight models are now used in an estimated 80% of startups and have closed the gap to frontier proprietary models to just 4-6 months. Directly relevant to model-selection decisions for SaidnDone and to any client engagement where cost or data-sovereignty rules out closed APIs.

## 3. Applied AI, Engineering & Customer-Facing Deployment

- **[Prompt caching in agents](https://earendil.com/posts/prompt-caching/)** [article] — A changed tool definition, model switch, or provider routing decision can silently blow up your cache and turn a cheap incremental request into a full context replay. This is a direct, practical risk for anything you've built on the Anthropic/OpenAI APIs — worth an audit of SaidnDone's cache hit rate.
- **[Nate Jones: Gumroad let a customer approve its code — here's where your agent should stop](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/first-ai-agent-use-case)** [video, ~21 min] — A concrete case study on scoping the first production agent: find the support/ops problem your team keeps repairing by hand, and define the exact point where the agent should hand off to a human. This is FDE playbook material — how to pick deployment #1 at a client.

Widens the "which model for which task" decision matrix inside the Microsoft stack.

## 5. AI Policy & Deployment Risk

- **[Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta lead ~25-company letter against "premature restrictions" on open-weight AI](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/24/nvidia-microsoft-meta-open-weight-ai-models.html)** [article] — Signers include Palantir, IBM, Hugging Face, Mistral, and (after the fact) OpenAI; Anthropic, Google, and Amazon are notably absent. Directly relevant to what you're allowed to ship into a client environment if US open-weight policy tightens or loosens.
- **[UK HM Treasury publishes Financial Services AI Adoption Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-plan-financial-services)** [article] — Published July 14; recommends designating major AI/cloud providers as "critical third parties" by end of 2026 and proposes a framework for agentic payments (liability, identity checks for autonomous agents). Pair this with the EU AI Act's August 2 deadline for high-risk finance-sector systems — both are live constraints on any AI deployment touching banking or insurance clients.
- **[Nate Jones: your company blocked ChatGPT for sensitive files — now what?](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/use-ai-sensitive-files)** [video, ~14 min] — The real problem isn't the "don't upload sensitive data" policy, it's that nobody tells employees how to actually get the work done under it. Practical framing for the shadow-IT conversation you'll have with any client's IT/security team.
