## 2. Frontier Research
- **[Eliminating nondeterminism in LLM inference with integer-only arithmetic](https://nathan.rs/posts/detllm/)** [article] — Floating-point non-associativity as the root of run-to-run variance, and integer addition as a fix. Reproducibility matters for audit trails in regulated client deployments.
- **[Chinese frontier models carry fingerprints of Blackwell GPUs](https://unemploymentlabs.com/checkpoint-forensics)** [article] — Forensic analysis suggests DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Ant trained on export-controlled chips. Source is a niche one and the post itself says it can't prove export-control violations. Matters for vendor-risk questions when clients ask about open-weight Chinese models.
- **[How agent anomaly detection scores on benchmarks we didn't write](https://developers.googleblog.com/how-agent-anomaly-detection-scores-on-benchmarks-we-didnt-write/)** [article] — Flags 87% of harmful executed instructions, weak on poisoned memory, up to 16% false alarms. A useful frame for the "how do you monitor agents in prod" interview question.
